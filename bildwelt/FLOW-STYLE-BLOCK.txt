Clean Animated Biology Illustration: simplified but recognizable anatomy, smooth organic forms, clean outlines, soft gradients, subtle depth, bright calm backgrounds, clear biological colors, low complexity, one dominant biological idea per frame.

No photoreal medical imagery, no medical CGI, no glossy 3D, no feature-animation/Pixar look, no toy/kids-book look, no corporate infographic, no clutter, no gore, no random icons, no unnecessary text.

This exact style is inherited from the approved Object-Talk video 008 (Verdauung). Illustrations should look like frames from that same clear, bright, slightly dimensional educational biology series. Prefer mostly pale cream/light turquoise/light blue/mint backgrounds, anatomical structures in clean coral/teal/blue. 16:9, straightforward biological close-up, cutaway, cellular process or structure.
IMPORTANT: The subject of the episode does NOT define a new art style. For SLEEP, use night colors only as restrained contextual accents; no deep navy/purple background as the normal canvas, glowing floating brains, neon neuroscience wallpaper, comic/anime sleeper, Pixar character or sci-fi look.
The cover is NOT a style reference for other scenes. Never use the selected cover or a previous scene to override this channel-wide written style. Every scene independently follows the canonical style above.
Cover has exactly one visible text block: the German title ONLY. On normal scenes use at most two short German labels, only if needed, otherwise no text. Light background -> dark high-contrast text; dark background -> light text. Production IDs and file names are NEVER visible. If scene information is too dense, simplify it or split into more images.
