GLOBAL VISUAL DIRECTION

Use a modern stylized 2.5D / soft-3D educational illustration style. The images must look clearly animated and illustrated, not realistic. Use rounded simplified shapes, matte surfaces, soft gradients, subtle shadows, gentle depth and calm bright backgrounds. One teaching idea per frame. Keep biology simplified but recognizable.

Target look:
80% animated illustration / 20% soft-3D depth / 0% medical realism.

Semantic colors:
- oxygen = cyan / turquoise
- red blood cells = deep warm red
- blood vessels = coral red
- organs = peach / coral
- muscle cells = apricot / orange
- CO2 = soft violet
- ATP / energy = warm yellow-gold
- background = light cream / soft blue / soft beige

DO NOT USE:
photorealism, medical CGI, wet tissue, realistic microscopic texture, anatomy-software look, dark sci-fi worlds, cosmic backgrounds, human presenters, torso mannequins, crowded infographic layouts.
